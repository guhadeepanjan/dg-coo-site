Profile

An operator's profile, written in outcomes

Head of Operations at ICICI Bank Singapore — 21 years at one institution, across five markets, in the rooms where payments, controls and regulators meet.

Head of Operations at ICICI Bank Singapore, with 21 years at one institution across Singapore, the GCC, the UK, Canada and India. I run branch-wide banking operations and governance — retail, private banking, institutional, trade finance, corporate & commercial banking, and domestic and international payments — under MAS supervision, and I have led the programmes that modernised them: the MEPS+ migration to the new messaging standard with no client disruption, a Target Operating Model that cut turnaround times.

Before Singapore, I built ICICI's International Operations Centre in Hyderabad — the capability hub for the bank's international markets — and established its Centre of Excellence. Earlier, I was part of the team that conceptualised Money2India and designed its operations end to end, a platform that became a remittance channel under FATF, RBI and MAS standards.

I represented the bank on the MAS MindForge AI Governance Taskforce (2022–2025), and am a member of the ABS Trade Finance Registry workgroup. My certifications cover operational risk, business continuity, AML/KYC and trade-based financial crime compliance (CTFC; CDCS in progress), and I completed the ISB Certificate Programme in Leadership with AI (2025).

"Transformation that strengthens control instead of trading it away."

What I am known for: payments modernisation, regulatory reporting (MAS 610/643A), operational resilience and first-line governance, delivered across borders. Based in Singapore. Open to industry conversations — panels, working groups and the exchange of operating practice.
