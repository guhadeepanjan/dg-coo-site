The Remit

A remittance platform, built like a control system

Money2India's operating model was built from first principles: transaction intake, screening, routing, reconciliation and beneficiary payout — each stage designed with its controls inside it, not inspected in afterwards. That design choice is why the platform could scale to very high transaction volumes with near-perfect processing accuracy.

The compliance backbone was built against FATF, RBI and MAS requirements, with the bank's response to FATF inspections and MAS regulatory inspections led from this desk — and AML/CFT controls that held throughout the build and scale-up. Fraud-detection and beneficiary-validation controls materially reduced disputes.

Every later mandate in this career runs on the pattern set here: payments are a control discipline first and a technology story second. It is also the experience behind the payments writing — from ISO 20022 migration commentary to "The Last Border in Money Is About to Fall" on Substack.
