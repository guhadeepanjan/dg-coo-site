The Work

Four disciplines, one operating philosophy

Transformation that strengthens control instead of trading it away. Each discipline below is a body of operational work — and a body of writing.

Payments Modernisation

MEPS+ and the new payment rails

Led the MEPS+ migration to the new messaging standard as a first-wave bank in Singapore — no client disruption and materially faster payment processing — across MEPS+, SWIFT MX and gpi, FAST, GIRO and correspondent banking.

Trade Finance Operations

Trade, run as a control discipline

Letters of credit, guarantees, documentary collections and discounting at branch scale, with a core contribution to the ABS Trade Finance Registry — and a writing desk that covers everything from LC workflows to Islamic trade funding.

Compliance & AML Operations

First-line governance that holds

KYC/AML operations, sanctions and screening governance, MAS 610/643A reporting, FATCA and CRS embedded into daily workflows — the operational machinery behind a sustained record of clean regulatory outcomes.

AI in Regulated Operations

Artificial intelligence, with named owners

Bank representative on the MAS MindForge AI Governance Taskforce; ISB Leadership in AI graduate; a target operating model built on RPA and machine learning that cut turnaround times materially while tightening controls.
