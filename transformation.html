Transformation Portfolio

Change that lands — without breaking the bank

Four programmes, one pattern: governance first, cutover discipline always, and outcomes measured in processing speed, control strength and cost — not in launch announcements.

Case 01 · Singapore
Payments modernisation

The MEPS+ migration to the new messaging standard

Led the branch's migration on MEPS+ to the new global messaging standard, as a first-wave bank in Singapore — the national payment system moving to the global messaging standard, with every client file, cut-off and reconciliation in scope.

  • No client disruption through cutover; a clean regulatory outcome
  • Materially faster payment processing on the new standard
  • Among the first banks in Singapore live on MEPS+ under the new standard
Case 02 · Singapore
Target Operating Model

A Target Operating Model built on RPA & machine learning

Redesigned how branch operations run — automation and machine learning applied to the workflows where controls could be strengthened at the same time, not traded away for speed.

  • Turnaround time materially reduced
  • Operational-loss incidents reduced
  • Operating expenditure reduced; a large share of manual workflows eliminated
Case 03 · Hyderabad
Shared services build

Building the International Operations Centre

Consolidated geography-based processing centres into one hub for markets across four continents within a single year — onboarding, payments, clearing and customer-records towers under one operating standard.

  • A step-change in productivity; operational incidents materially reduced
  • Centre of Excellence: a large share of FTE redeployed to higher-value work
  • Audits across five regulators coordinated with clean outcomes
Case 04 · Mumbai
Platform build

Money2India: an operating model from a blank page

Designed the end-to-end operating model for a greenfield remittance platform — intake, screening, routing, reconciliation, payout — with its FATF/RBI/MAS compliance backbone built in from day one.

  • Scaled to very high transaction volumes and flagship-level annual flows
  • Near-perfect processing accuracy; disputes materially reduced
  • AML/CFT controls that held throughout the build and scale-up
"The pattern across all four: the control framework is designed first, and the technology is made to fit it — never the other way round."
